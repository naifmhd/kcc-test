Our story

A small shop with a big love for fresh food

Fresh Basket started as a single fruit stand on the corner. Today we're a family-run grocery that still hand-picks every crate and knows most customers by name.

We work directly with local farms and bakers, so the food on our shelves is fresher, tastier and kinder to the planet.
